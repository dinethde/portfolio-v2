CI runs axe in WebKit on Linux, and it fails the `target-size` and `target-offset` rules (WCAG 2.2, 24px) on the desktop menu links (`MenuLink`) and the footer card's links (`FooterCard`, the `rounded-4 font-rounded text-p2` links). Make each one's tap area at least 24px high without moving the visible text: for example `inline-flex items-center min-h-24`, with a negative margin that only cancels the extra height if the layout would shift (D-57 allows that). The menu link's focus outline (D-46) must still hug the label as Figma draws it. Compare with Figma at 1440 (menu) and at 390 and 1440 (footer): nothing visible may move.

Separately, the test "on a phone the menu button opens the panel from the keyboard" fails in WebKit on CI with `aria-expanded` still `false`: it presses Enter before React has hydrated. Make the test wait for hydration before pressing Enter, for example by retrying focus + Enter inside `expect(...).toPass()` until `aria-expanded` is `true`. Don't change the menu itself for this.
